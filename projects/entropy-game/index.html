1. The Gambler's Fallacy (Alternation Bias)

Humans switch between digits far too frequently (often ~65% of the time in binary, instead of the 50% theoretical expectation). We intuitively feel that after a '1', a '0' is "overdue".

2. Streak / Run Starvation

In a true random binary sequence of 60 bits, there is a >90% chance of a streak of 4 or more identical symbols ($E[\max L] \approx \log_2 N$). Humans actively avoid long streaks, starving the sequence of natural clusters.

3. Sequential Martingales (Solving Peeking)

Evaluating randomness after every key with standard p-values causes false-positive explosion. This app implements Test Martingales and Ville's Inequality, guaranteeing that false rejections are strictly bounded by $\alpha$ over indefinitely long typing.

4. Universal Online Predictor

An adaptive multi-order Markov mixture (PPM / Context Tree) learns your personal typing grammar in real-time. If it can guess your next key with >50% accuracy (or >10% in decimal), your sequence is compressed and non-random.
